About Our Bakery

Francks Crub Bakery started in 2026 and is run by a small family team. We offer fresh pandesal, ensaymada, cupcakes, and cakes for birthdays and special occasions.

Our goal is to serve delicious baked products and make every celebration more special. We believe that every bite should be made with love!
